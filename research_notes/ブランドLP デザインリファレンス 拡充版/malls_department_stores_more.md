## Q1. International facilities not yet covered: which have strong visuals, credited design, awards or documented results?

### Takeaway
The strongest new international references are **Korean**. Shinsegae Gangnam was the first Korean store to pass ₩3 trillion (2023) and reached a reported ₩3.67tn in 2025, helped by its food-hall and "House of Shinsegae" renewals (BLT Built Design Award 2025). The Hyundai Seoul reached ₩1tn in 33 months, the fastest in Korea, without Hermès, Louis Vuitton or Chanel, on the strength of a Burdifilek interior and an indoor forest. Lotte Jamsil has also passed ₩3tn. For glamour and credited design, the best new cases are **SKP-S** (Gentle Monster × Sybarite), **Printemps New York** (Laura Gonzalez, 2025), **Liberty London** (Pentagram identity, 2020) and **Miami Design District** (Made Thought, creative partner for 10+ years). For sales density, **Bal Harbour Shops** is the US benchmark (~$3,000+/sq ft). **Taikoo Li Sanlitun** gives a documented "repositioning → sales jump" case: +63% after the Hermès-anchored upgrade, per press summaries that still need checking against Swire filings. Again I found no web-specific award (Awwwards/FWA/Webby) for any of these facility sites.

#### N1. Shinsegae Gangnam (신세계 강남점), Seoul. Korea's top-selling department store
- **Official URL:** https://www.shinsegae.com/ **[URL unverified]**
- **Client:** Shinsegae Inc.
- **Credits (space):** **House of Shinsegae**, a 7,273 m² F&B space styled as a luxury hotel inside the store. Interior by **A Work of Substance** (lead designers Maxime Dautresme and Seoyoon K) — [IADS](https://www.iads.org/member/news/shinsegae-unveils-house-of-shinsegae-luxury-hotel-experience-inside-department-store); [BLT Built Design Awards 2025 winner page](https://bltawards.com/winner/blt/2025/22213/0). One summary says it was inspired by flagship concepts such as House of Dior (same IADS item). I found no design credit for **Sweet Park**, the dessert hall. CallisonRTKL lists a Shinsegae Department Store project but gives no details in the snippet — [CallisonRTKL](https://callisonrtkl.com/projects/shinsegae-department-store).
- **Awards:** **BLT Built Design Awards 2025**, Retail & Shop Interiors (winner), for House of Shinsegae — [BLT](https://bltawards.com/winner/blt/2025/22213/0). Older: **World Retail Awards 2013 "Store Design of the Year"** (over 1,200 m²) went to Shinsegae via JHP. This is outside the 2018–26 window and is listed for context only — [Moodie Davitt](https://moodiedavittreport.com/jhp-wins-store-design-of-the-year-for-shinsegae-department-store-project).
- **Results:**
  - **First Korean store to pass ₩3 trillion** in annual sales (≈$2.3bn, 2023) — [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/shinsegaes-gangnam-branch-becomes-first-korean-store-to-crack-3-trillion-won/11369126); [KED Global](https://www.kedglobal.com/newsView/ked202312210006).
  - Reported series: ₩2.494tn (2021), ₩2.8398tn (2022), ₩3.1025tn (2023), ₩3.3269tn (2024). **2025 transaction volume ₩3.6717tn**, and the store is now "challenging ₩4tn" — [Asia Economy, Aug 2026](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026081917044776015). The 2021–24 series came through the search summary alongside these articles. I could not confirm which article it comes from, so verify it before quoting.
  - **Sweet Park** (5,300 m², ~43 dessert brands, "Korea's largest dessert zone") was the first stage of the first food-hall overhaul since 2009 — [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/shinsegaes-sweet-park-is-koreas-new-mecca-for-dessert-lovers/12422496). It averaged **1.34M visitors a month**, as cited in Shinsegae's Daegu roll-out story — [Asia Economy, Jul 2024](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2024072511370092470). The renewed food hall passed **10M cumulative customers within a year** — [Asia Economy, Aug 2026](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026080521592990277). Separately, the store's "biggest renewal in 12 years" was aimed at ultra-high-net-worth shoppers — [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/shinsegae-focuses-on-ultra-rich-with-biggest-renewal-in-12-years/12425976).
- **Visual/UX traits:** A hotel-lobby metaphor for F&B (House of Shinsegae) — [IADS](https://www.iads.org/member/news/shinsegae-unveils-house-of-shinsegae-luxury-hotel-experience-inside-department-store). The website's floor guide and brand directory were **[unverified — not inspected]**.
- **Borrow (inference):** Name the "house" sub-brands (House of Shinsegae, The Heritage) as chapters of the LP, each with its own mood, rather than a single flat brand list.
- **Image URL:** https://bltawards.com/winner/blt/2025/22213/0

#### N2. The Hyundai Seoul (더현대 서울), Yeouido, opened Feb 2021. Fastest store in Korea to ₩1 trillion
- **Official URL:** https://www.ehyundai.com/ **[URL unverified]**
- **Client:** Hyundai Department Store
- **Credits (space):** Architecture by **Rogers Stirk Harbour + Partners**. Design firm **Burdifilek** (Toronto) did the atriums and selected areas of floors 2–4 — [Retail Design Institute](https://www.retaildesigninstitute.org/post/the-hyundai-seoul-51st-international-design-competition-winner); [Burdifilek, "The Atriums"](https://burdifilek.com/project/the-hyundai-the-atriums). The Korea Herald says Hyundai worked with **nine global interior firms**, including Burdifilek, CallisonRTKL and CMK — [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/2566180). Attribution differs between these sources.
- **Awards:** **Retail Design Institute, 51st International Design Competition, winner** — [RDI](https://www.retaildesigninstitute.org/post/the-hyundai-seoul-51st-international-design-competition-winner).
- **Results:**
  - **₩1 trillion annual sales in 33 months (Dec 2023)**, the fastest of any Korean branch and 26 months faster than the previous record. This was achieved **without Hermès, Louis Vuitton or Chanel**. Hyundai credits exclusive K-fashion brands and pop-ups. **72.8% of foreign sales came from customers in their 20s–30s** — [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/the-hyundai-seoul-becomes-fastest-store-to-hit-1-trillion-won-milestone/12469235); [IADS](https://www.iads.org/member/news/the-hyundai-seoul-becomes-fastest-store-to-hit-1-trillion-won-milestone).
  - **100M visitors in 30 months** — [KED Global, Aug 2023](https://amp.kedglobal.com/newsAmp/ked202308280002). Foreign visitors came from 80 countries in 2023 — [Asia Economy](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2023112309355757271).
- **Visual/UX traits:** "Sounds Forest" is a 3,300 m² indoor park on 5F with real grass, flowers and about 30 trees — [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/2566180). Burdifilek's interiors are described as sculptural, with "pops of blue & yellow" — [designboom](https://designboom.com/?p=889718); [Yellowtrace](https://yellowtrace.com.au/burdifilek-the-hyundai-seoul-luxury-retail-interiors/). Website UX **[unverified — not inspected]**.
- **Borrow (inference):** Proof that "experience-first" content (indoor forest, pop-ups, exclusive young brands) can outsell a top-3 maison line-up. An LP can lead with experiences and discoveries, not just the logo wall.
- **Image URL:** https://yellowtrace.com.au/burdifilek-the-hyundai-seoul-luxury-retail-interiors/ (alt: https://burdifilek.com/project/the-hyundai-the-atriums)

#### N4. Printemps New York, One Wall Street, opened 20/21 Mar 2025
- **Official URL:** https://www.printemps.com/ (NY section) **[URL unverified]**. Group announcement — [Groupe Printemps](https://assets-prod.groupe-printemps.com/en/article/printemps-new-yorks-opening).
- **Client:** Printemps (Groupe Printemps)
- **Credits (space):** Interior designer **Laura Gonzalez**, appointed 2022 for a "Paris meets New York" direction. **Studio Pierre Marie** reinterpreted Printemps' stained-glass heritage for NY — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/retail/discover-the-new-printemps-department-store-in-new-york/2025032080721); [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/printemps-new-york-one-wall-street-flagship-03-20-2025/); [Hospitality Design](https://hospitalitydesign.com/news/retail-commercial/printemps-new-york/).
- **Awards:** none found. VMSD featured it in a Jul/Aug 2025 issue — [VMSD "Paris Springs Eternal"](https://editions.mydigitalpublication.com/article/PARIS+SPRINGS+ETERNAL/5014163/849913/article.html).
- **Results:** none published. Opening dates differ between sources (20 vs 21 Mar 2025).
- **Visual/UX traits:** It is planned as a **Parisian pied-à-terre or Haussmann triplex apartment**, with the landmark **Red Room** (the former Irving Trust banking hall) kept intact using freestanding fixtures. Paris Art Nouveau is layered over NY Art Deco, and the store is laid out as **ten distinctly designed sections**. It is positioned as "not a department store", with hospitality, gastronomy, culture, design and retail together — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/retail/discover-the-new-printemps-department-store-in-new-york/2025032080721); [6sqft](https://www.6sqft.com/printemps-opens-in-nyc-one-wall-street/).
- **Borrow (inference):** An "apartment of rooms" information architecture, where each brand cluster is a named room (salon, boudoir, bar). This is a strong alternative to an A–Z grid for a curated LP.
- **Image URL:** https://www.designboom.com/architecture/printemps-new-york-one-wall-street-flagship-03-20-2025/

#### N5. SKP-S, Beijing, opened Dec 2019 opposite SKP. Designed with Gentle Monster
- **Official URL:** none confirmed **[URL unverified]**. Database entry — [IADS: SKP-S](https://www.iads.org/department-stores/skp-s).
- **Client:** SKP (Beijing Hualian SKP)
- **Credits (space):** Co-created by **Gentle Monster** (creative concept) and **Sybarite** (architecture/interiors) — [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/skp-s-beijing-sybarite-gentle-monster-02-07-2020/); [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/sneak-peak-inside-of-skp-and-gentle-monsters-newly-created-mall/). - **Visual/UX traits:** Four floors set in a stylised alternate universe. The ground floor is dark, with black opaque surfaces and spot lighting. - **Borrow (inference):** A narrative "world" (sci-fi or Mars) as the connecting thread across maisons. It is the most theatrical precedent for scroll-driven storytelling with dark UI and spotlit product.

### Inferences
- The renewals that worked combined **a named, photogenic destination zone** (Sweet Park, House of Shinsegae, Sounds Forest, Hermès flagship at Sanlitun North) with **a trade-mix change**. For an LP, a "featured zone / featured house" module with its own visual world looks like the right analogue.
- Japanese results are mostly macro-driven (inbound tourism and the weak yen). Credit them as a market tailwind, not as a design effect.
