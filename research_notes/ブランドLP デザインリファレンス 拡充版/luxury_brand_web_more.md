# Award-winning and visually outstanding luxury brand web and campaign work: second pass (NEW references only; fashion, jewelry, watches, beauty, fragrance, hospitality; 2020–2026)

## How to read these notes

**Method.** This pass used about 49 web searches. WebFetch was not used (blocked by network policy), so everything below comes from search-result snippets.
- Awwwards award tiers and dates are as the Awwwards listing pages appeared in search results.
- Visual traits are only what a listing, case study or article states.
- Anything else is marked **[INF]** (inference) or **[verify]** (needs a look at the page).
- "Image URL (seen)" means the exact URL appeared in search results. "(constructed, verify)" means it follows the Awwwards `/sites/<slug>` pattern but was not seen.

**Exclusions.** These are deliberately left out because the earlier passes already cover them:
- Cartier series, LV Collectibles, Chanel Boutons, Hermès Tutti Twilly, Loewe × AKQA, Gucci Vault/Town, Dioriviera, Dior by Starck, Bulgari 130, Rimowa Unique, POLA 2029, Omega.
- Miu Miu, Maison Alaïa, The Maison Dior, LVMH "Maison of All Victories", Tengile MalaMala.

**Overall headline.**
- **Cannes Luxury Lions (2024–2026) reward campaigns, not websites.** The winners are a social shorts series (LOEWE × Suna Fujita), a documentary (Lancôme × Aya Nakamura), a platform hand-over to artisans (Bottega for Bottegas), a sponsorship (LVMH Paris 2024), fashion/hospitality collaborations (Ritz-Carlton × Late Checkout), a film (Moncler) and an entertainment tie-in (Tiffany × Netflix).
- **Web-craft awards in luxury cluster on Awwwards SOTD/HM.** New maisons found: Boucheron, Dolce&Gabbana Beauty, Loro Piana, TAG Heuer, Breguet, Issey Miyake Parfums, Guerlain, Aesop, and the Balenciaga heritage sites. New luxury-hospitality winners: Primland, Badrutt's Palace, Velaa and Vander.
- **The most "multi-brand LP"-relevant new finds** are:
  - **Bottega for Bottegas**: a luxury house lends its e-commerce and windows to many independent shops. It is the only one here with sales and traffic figures claimed in its award entry.
  - **Loro Piana "Workshop of Wonders" at Harrods**: a web game tied to a physical store takeover, with QR points and a digital map.
  - **Aesop home-fragrance "constellation"**: a multi-product universe, with the agency claiming +400% sales.

### Inferences
- **[INF] What the Cannes juries reward:** platform-lending, collaboration and culture (Bottega for Bottegas, Ritz × Late Checkout, Tiffany × Netflix). This suggests a multi-brand LP earns "award-grade" credibility through its **concept**, such as a venue lending its stage to each maison or a shared cultural theme, more than through UI polish.
- **[INF] Bottega for Bottegas is the closest conceptual analogue to a "many boutiques in one place" page.** A luxury house gives its digital shelf to other makers, and the entry claims traffic and sales uplift. A venue LP could use the same narrative frame ("our stage, their craft").
- **[INF] The "windows" motif recurs:** Bottega windows, Tiffany Landmark windows, Loro Piana's 36 Harrods windows (Q2). A "window-by-window" scroll for each boutique is a credible LP device.

### Inferences
- **[INF] Gamification persists in 2024–2025 luxury winners:**
  - Boucheron: 10+ mini-games
  - Loro Piana: a treasure hunt of 10 symbols
  - D&G: scent-blending
  - Issey Miyake: salt-sculpture crafting
