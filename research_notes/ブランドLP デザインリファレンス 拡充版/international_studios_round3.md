# International (non-Japanese) agencies and boutique studios, round 3: remaining names from the brief, Awwwards annual winners, and Asian studios with luxury, fashion, beauty and retail work (multi-brand luxury-boutique LP; airports out of scope)

> **How these notes were gathered.**
> - I ran 45 web searches, which is this researcher's share of the shared budget. As instructed, I did not use WebFetch.
> - Every fact below comes from search-result snippets or the search tool's summaries of them, not from full page reads. Where a claim rests on a summary or a single source, the entry says so.
> - I filled nothing in from memory. Where my background knowledge differs from a source, the entry flags it rather than "correcting" it.
>
> **Scope.**
> - Studios verified in `空港ブティックLP デザインリファレンス/international_agencies.md` (round 1) and `ブランドLP デザインリファレンス 拡充版/international_studios_more.md` (round 2) are not repeated.
> - A few of them (Lusion, Cosmic Shelter, Pentagram, Base Design) reappear only where this round found a new project or fact.
> - No Japanese studios and no airport projects are included.
>
> **Labels.**
> - Type: [GLOBAL] global brand/design agency · [DIGITAL] digital/web studio · [LUXURY] luxury specialist · [BOUTIQUE] independent boutique · [SPACE] retail/interior/architecture · [AD] advertising/communications.
> - "Image URL" is the public page that best shows the work for a later screenshot. Every one of them appeared in search results.
>
> **Headline result.** Most of the remaining "famous agency" names in the brief have **no public luxury, fashion or beauty case** in the sources found. The strongest new material came from three places:
> 1. **The Korean luxury department-store ecosystem.** The Hyundai and Shinsegae draw in JHP, thonik, Universal Everything, Studio fnt, Works, Major Minority, Burdifilek, Moonwalker Studio and Mucca.
> 2. **FutureBrand's retail arm, UXUS.** Its work includes Selfridges Beauty Workshop, Qela and Peninsula Boutique.
> 3. **Paris and Italian luxury-digital specialists.** Disko (Kering), Cosmic Shelter (LVMH, Cartier) and Adoratorio and Lusion (Max Mara).
>
> In total, about 30 studios have at least one verified fashion, beauty, retail or luxury-destination credit. Only about 15 have verified *luxury* credits (see the table in KQ4).

## Key Question 2: Who won Awwwards Agency, Studio and Site of the Year in 2021–2025, and which winners have luxury clients?

### Takeaway
The Awwwards record could only be partly reconstructed, and sources conflict. Awwwards' "Annual Awards YYYY" page labels and the year of the ceremony appear to be offset by a year. That is my inference, and it would explain most of the conflicts.

Best-supported winners:
- **Agency of the Year:** Locomotive for 2020 and 2021 (Wikipedia mirror).
- **Studio of the Year:**
  - Green Chameleon, 2021 (Wikipedia mirror)
  - Obys, 2023 (self-claim)
  - Malvah, 2025 (LBB)
- **Site of the Year:**
  - KPR by Resn, 2022 label (press release March 2023)
  - Igloo Inc by abeto, 2024 (Awwwards collection page)
  - Mana Yerba Mate, 2023 (one collection snippet)

### Inferences
- For a "many luxury boutiques in one place" LP, **The Hyundai** is the best Asian case to screenshot, because it shows a layered identity stack:
  1. a master brand system (Studio fnt over the Base Design logotype);
  2. a flagship sub-identity (Works' The Hyundai Seoul);
  3. event and anniversary graphics (Major Minority);
  4. moving-image façades (Universal Everything);
  5. zone interiors (Burdifilek).

  An LP can mirror this with a master frame plus per-zone and per-event modules.
- **Shinsegae's** Red Dot 2026 Myeong-dong rebrand and Moonwalker's seasonal "Blooming Playlist" show the idea of a "seasonal campaign skin" over a stable master brand. That suits an LP that rotates its featured maisons by season.
- **thonik's outlet work** is the only Asian retail project with a reported business result (+66% opening-day sales, self-reported). It is outlet, not full-price luxury.

- **Not searched (budget):** Studio SML, Bravo (Singapore), Haus Nowhere, other Shanghai luxury-mall branding studios, and the SKP identity.

### Inferences
Together with rounds 1 and 2, round 3 adds three LP patterns:
1. **Master brand plus layered sub-identities** (The Hyundai stack). This suits an LP that must stay coherent across dozens of maisons.
2. **Seasonal skin** (Shinsegae "Blooming Playlist"). Rotate the hero art direction by season while the frame stays fixed.
3. **Moving-image façade hero** (Universal Everything × The Hyundai), built in a performant way (Lusion's pre-baked WebGL for MaxMara), so it loads on mobile.

The multi-brand beauty-hall logic (UXUS × Selfridges) and the heritage-entrance storytelling (Printemps New York's Red Room) are good models for a "beauty floor" section and a "destination story" intro.
