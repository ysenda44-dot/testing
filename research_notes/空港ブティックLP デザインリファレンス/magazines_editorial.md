## Q1. International fashion/luxury magazines known for art direction: which won design awards recently, and who designs them?

### Takeaway
The verified award trail for luxury/fashion titles is thinner than expected. T: The New York Times Style Magazine has an SPD Gold (Fashion/Beauty, SPD 54, Creative Director Patrick Li). Wallpaper*'s art-direction lineage has PPA/BSME honours. Fantastic Man / The Gentlewoman (Jop van Bennekom) appear in the D&AD archive and are repeatedly studied by magCulture for their redesigns. Holiday (Atelier Franck Durand) is the reference for a "travel + luxury" art-directed relaunch. 2024–2026 SPD/Stack winner lists could not be retrieved.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **T Magazine → LP hero and section system.** An SPD Gold fashion title with a 2018 redesign is a reference for an oversized serif masthead over full-bleed fashion photography, with a clear section rhythm. On the LP, this can become a cover-like hero ("AIRPORT BOUTIQUES — Autumn/Winter 2026") followed by issue-style sections.
- **Fantastic Man / The Gentlewoman → typographic restraint.** These titles redesign through tiny type and cover changes rather than visual noise ("subtle and indeed drastic"). That suits a multi-brand page, which has to frame very different brand identities in one neutral house style without competing with the logos.
- **Wallpaper* → the "Design Awards / Best of" format.** An annual "awards" or "best city" package maps well to an LP module such as "Editors' Picks at Terminal X" or "Best New Boutiques".
- **Holiday → travel-luxury tone.** It is a direct analogue for an airport: playful travel graphics, experimental photography and bilingual text (FR/EN), which suggests a JP/EN or multi-language layout built into the design.
- **Awards as credibility signals.** Only T Magazine (SPD Gold) and Wallpaper*'s designers (PPA/BSME) have verifiable award citations in this set. The writer should not claim awards for other titles without checking.

- No verified data was retrieved for **Vogue (any edition), Harper's Bazaar, W, Kinfolk, Cereal, Apartamento, Self Service, Purple, AnOther, System, 032c, Document Journal, Monocle, Numéro**.

## Q2. Brand-published magazines and books (Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Prada, Bottega Veneta, Gucci, Aesop, MR PORTER, PORTER, SSENSE, Mytheresa)

### Takeaway
Luxury houses run large-circulation owned media: Le Monde d'Hermès (about 600,000 copies, over 50 years old), LV The Book (reported 1 million copies) and the Louis Vuitton City Guides (since 1998, 43 destinations in the 2026 edition). Newer formats are more experimental: Bottega Veneta's quarterly digital *Issue* (2021), the 500-page Acne Paper relaunch (2024) and Chanel's 250-page *Arts & Culture Magazine*. Net-a-Porter's PORTER is the key commercial proof point for "shoppable print": shoppable on every page, with 78% of readers reported to interact with the shoppable elements.

### Inferences
- **Le Monde d'Hermès → "rotating art direction" and commissioned illustration.** An airport LP could refresh its hero art direction each season, with a guest illustrator drawing the terminal. This keeps a permanent multi-brand page feeling new without redesigning the system. The roaming physical kiosk also suggests tying the LP to an on-site printed edition.
- **LV City Guides → colour-coding and luggage-tag identity.** "A colour per city" maps directly to "a colour per terminal/zone" or per brand category. The luggage-label motif is a natural airport metaphor (boarding pass / bag-tag labels for brand cards).
- **Bottega Veneta Issue → the "issue drop" cadence.** Releasing the LP's editorial layer quarterly, in line with in-store collection launches, gives a reason to return. Guest cover designers (e.g. Okuyama) show how a single cover image can carry a whole digital issue.
- **Acne Paper / Chanel Arts & Culture → the "object-like" long scroll.** A 250–500-page book-magazine hybrid suggests a long-scroll LP with chaptering and generous pacing rather than a dense grid.
- **PORTER → the core commercial pattern for the LP.** Every editorial image carries product credits linked to a purchase or reservation path. This is the strongest evidence that shoppable editorial engages users (78% interaction, as self-reported). For an airport, "buy" can become "reserve and collect at gate / view boutique location / opening hours".
- **MR PORTER → two speeds.** An always-on stream (Journal) plus a curated "best-of" edition (Post) suggests an LP with a curated hero edit plus a continuously updated "New arrivals in the terminal" feed.

### Gaps
- No data retrieved for **Prada, Gucci, Aesop, SSENSE editorial, Mytheresa**, or for Chanel/Prada house magazines beyond Arts & Culture.
- No design-studio credit was found for the core LV City Guide layout or for Le Monde d'Hermès across issues, beyond the FW2020 credit.
- No verified award (Content Marketing Awards, D&AD, Cannes) was found for any brand magazine except the Cannes Lions archive entry for "Louis Vuitton Guides" (level unknown).
- The current (2024–2026) PORTER print status and circulation could not be confirmed; sources conflict.
- UNVERIFIED leads: Aesop's editorial platform "The Fabulist"; SSENSE's long-form editorial section; Prada's cultural publications. Verify before use.

## Q3. Department store / mall magazines (Harrods, Selfridges, Bergdorf Goodman, Galeries Lafayette, GINZA SIX, Isetan, Hankyu, LUMINE)

### Takeaway
Harrods is the most documented case. Its magazine began as a Condé Nast contract title (Condé Nast worked for Harrods and Selfridges from 1984), moved to River Publishing, and is now an in-house operation producing more than 15 annual publications (Harrods Magazine, Home, Estates, Harrods Man, plus a Harrods Magazine Arabia edition). It was relaunched under editor-in-chief Tiffanie Darke in 2018. No Harrods ad-revenue figures were found. GINZA SIX publishes a "GINZA SIX magazine" (2017 onward) inside a Kenya Hara-branded identity.

### Cited Findings

**Harrods Magazine** (department-store magazine, monthly; Harrods, London). URL: https://www.harrods.com (Harrods Magazine / editorial)
- Condé Nast entered contract publishing "as long ago as 1984, with magazines for Harrods and Selfridges". [Marketing Week: Glossy publishers go 'own-label'](https://www.marketingweek.com/glossy-publishers-go-own-label/)
- Harrods later moved its contract publishing account from Condé Nast to River Publishing (undated in the snippet). [Campaign](https://www.campaignlive.co.uk/article/media-harrods-moves-contract-publishing-account/567342)
- In **2018** Harrods hired **Tiffanie Darke** (from A+E Networks) as editor-in-chief in an "editorial strategy shift". The monthly Harrods Magazine, the quarterly Harrods Man titles and social channels were to be relaunched within six months. [FashionNetwork](https://pl.fashionnetwork.com/news/Harrods-names-editor-in-chief-as-part-of-editorial-strategy-shift,1024398.html)

- **Harrods Magazine Arabia** launched in the UAE, edited by Jan Masters with Deborah Bee as editor-in-chief. [Campaign Middle East](https://campaignme.com/harrods-magazine-arabia-launches/)

## Q6. Which editorial layout patterns translate well to web LPs?

### Takeaway
The evidence points to six transferable patterns:
1. A cover-like hero with a strong masthead
2. Issue/season drops timed to in-store launches (Bottega)
3. Shoppable product credits on editorial imagery (PORTER)
4. A brand/shopping directory as a distinct section (Dubai Voyager)
5. Colour-coding by place (LV City Guides)
6. Rotating guest art direction or illustration (Le Monde d'Hermès, Bottega covers)

Mobile-first, multilingual adaptations (ANA, JAL) show how print layouts are re-flowed for phones.

### Cited Findings
- **Shoppable credits.** PORTER made every page shoppable via app scan-to-product links; 78% of readers were reported to interact. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/net-porter-built-digital-shopping-experience-print-magazine/)
- **Print-to-screen layout.** PORTER's tablet edition used single-column grids, many full-page images, generous white space and serif display type. [FIPP](https://www.fipp.com/news/features/the-perfect-digital-fashion-magazine-experience)
- **Directory section.** Dubai Voyager carried a 16-page Dubai Duty Free Shopping Directory. [Enigma Global](https://www.enigmaglobal.com/portfolio/dubai-voyager-magazine/)
- **Colour-per-place system.** The 2019 LV City Guides refresh gave each city its own colour; the earlier design drew on LV luggage labels. [Livres Hebdo](https://www.livreshebdo.fr/article/voyager-encore-plus-chic-avec-louis-vuitton); [Luxe Magazine](https://luxe-magazine.com/en/article/4405-vuitton_and_the_city.html?r=17)
- **Issue cadence tied to retail.** Bottega Veneta's quarterly digital Issue was timed to coincide with collection launches in store. [FashionNetwork](https://ca.fashionnetwork.com/news/Bottega-Veneta-launches-quarterly-digital-journal,1291631.html)
- **Rotating art direction.** Le Monde d'Hermès changes its art direction yearly. [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/fashion/fashion-houses-take-on-publishing) *(attribution uncertain)*
- **Guest cover designer.** Taiki Okuyama designed Bottega's Issue 03 cover. [Numéro](https://numero.com/en/fashion/graphic-designer-okuyama-taiki-talks-about-his-collaboration-with-bottega-veneta/)
- **Always-on plus best-of.** MR PORTER's Journal publishes around the clock, while the Post compiles the best of it. [MR PORTER](https://www.mrporter.com/en-th/journal/lifestyle/introducing-all-new-journal-daily-editorial-2020-1033908); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/lifestyle/fashion/the-directory-5-years-of-mr-porter-5-menswear-lessons-1.1683248)
- **Mobile re-flow and multilingual.** ANA's 翼の王国 was redesigned for smartphone reading in three languages (2021). [Impress Watch](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1308491.html)
- **Minimal photography-led layouts in travel.** Open Skies' 2017 redesign: contemporary photography, clean minimal design, warm palette. [IMM International](https://www.imm-international.com/emirates-inflight-magazine-advertising)
- **Book-like long form.** Acne Paper's 500-page book/magazine hybrid (2024). [Istituto Marangoni](https://www.istitutomarangoni.com/en/maze35/fashion/why-luxury-fashion-brands-are-launching-magazines-and-what-it-says-about-culture)
- **Award / "best of" packaging.** The Wallpaper* Design Awards (20th edition, 2024) include "Best City". [Wallpaper*](https://wallpaper.com/design-interiors/february-2024-issue-read-more)

### Inferences
Proposed LP module map, with a print precedent for each:

| LP module | Print precedent | How to adapt for a multi-brand airport boutique LP |
|---|---|---|
| **Cover hero** | T Magazine, Fantastic Man, Holiday | A full-bleed seasonal image with a large masthead-style wordmark, issue/season label ("AW26"), and three or four cover lines that jump to sections ("New: Hermès Terminal 2", "The Watch Edit", "Gifts under ¥50,000"). |
| **Contents / "In this issue"** | All magazines | A sticky in-page navigation styled as a table of contents with page-like numbering (01 Feature / 02 Edits / 03 Directory / 04 Services). |
| **Feature story** | Le Monde d'Hermès, Casa BRUTUS 特集 | One long-form story per season (craft, a heritage object, a designer interview), with guest illustration or art direction rotated seasonally. |
| **The Edit / shopping page** | PORTER, department-store gift guides | Still-life product grids on white or colour fields with numbered product credits (Brand, Item, Price, Terminal/Gate). Tap a credit to see the boutique location, reserve, or check tax-free eligibility. |
| **Lookbook grid** | Fashion editorial | An asymmetric 2–3 column image grid with captions in the margins, giving brand campaigns equal weight while keeping one house style. |
| **Brand directory** | Dubai Voyager's 16-page directory | An A–Z or by-category directory with terminal/zone colour-coding (LV colour-per-city logic), hours and a map pin. |
| **City / terminal guide** | LV City Guides, POPEYE/TRANSIT | An illustrated terminal "walk" with numbered stops: boutiques, lounges, cafés. |
| **Issue drops** | Bottega Issue, MR PORTER Post | A quarterly editorial refresh aligned with in-store collection launches, with an archive of past "issues". |
| **Multilingual layer** | Holiday (FR/EN), ANA (JP/EN/ZH), BRUTUS/POPEYE EN | Language switching as a designed element, plus typography pairs that hold up across Japanese, English and Chinese. |

- **Visual guardrails from the evidence:**
  - Generous white space and serif display type (PORTER).
  - Minimal grid and warm palette (Open Skies).
  - Restrained house typography so that very different brand logos can coexist (Fantastic Man / Gentlewoman).

### Gaps
- No user-testing or conversion data was found comparing magazine-style LPs with standard directory LPs; PORTER's 78% interaction figure is self-reported and dates from 2014–2016.
- Page-level visual analysis (cover typography, specific grids) could not be done from primary images because fetching spreads was blocked. The visual descriptors above are limited to those stated in the sources; the module table is inference.
