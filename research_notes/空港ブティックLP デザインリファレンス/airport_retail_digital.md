# Airport retail / duty-free / airport luxury boutique digital media: design references (worldwide + Japan)

*Research conditions (read first): in this session, direct page fetching was blocked for every non-search host tried (moodiedavittreport.com, heathrow.com, changiairport.com, dubaidutyfree.com, narita-airport.jp, tokyo-haneda.com, extime.com, awwwards.com, web.archive.org). The shared web-search budget then ran out partway through, so the planned searches on Awwwards/Webby airport winners, Zurich The Circle, Istanbul/Unifree, King Power, Heinemann, Avolta and Shinsegae were never run. Everything below comes from search-result content: official pages that surfaced in the search index (Oct 2026), trade press and agency case pages. Page-level visual descriptions (typography, colour, motion) could NOT be checked first-hand and are listed as gaps. URL patterns and service descriptions found in search snippets are given where available. "Live status" means the URL surfaced in Oct-2026 search results. It does not mean the page was loaded.*

## Q1. Which airports / travel retailers have the most acclaimed luxury-shopping web experiences? (International catalogue)

### Takeaway
The strongest multi-brand airport luxury references are Heathrow, Extime/Paris Aéroport and Changi:
- **Heathrow:** heathrow.com was re-platformed by Publicis Sapient, with Reserve & Collect at boutique.heathrow.com. Heathrow won Skytrax's 2026 Best Airport Shopping award and has vendor-reported uplift of +29% revenue and +39% conversion.
- **Extime / Paris Aéroport:** Groupe ADP's luxury retail and hospitality brand runs a per-brand page system with terminal, hours and Click & Collect. It won at The Moodies 2025, and its spend per passenger reaches €87.60 in CDG T1.
- **Changi:** the site was personalised and redesigned by Tribal DDB, with iShopChangi by Tribal Worldwide. Changi was Skytrax World's Best Airport 2026.

Dubai Duty Free, Qatar Duty Free/Hamad and HKIA are best documented for sales and awards. No independent design award (Awwwards/Webby/D&AD) was found for any airport luxury LP.

### Inferences
- **For the LP's business case:** the defensible logic is that luxury-zone renovation and branding lift SPP (Extime), while an integrated digital directory with pre-order lifts conversion (Heathrow). Online sales share is small (about 3% at DXB), so LP KPIs should include store visits, pre-orders and coupon or member sign-ups.
